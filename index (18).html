A Grand Subramanyapura Landmark

South Bengaluru’s Destination For Bridal & Festive Elegance

At A-ONE Bangles & Fancy Store, we believe every bangle holds a spark of celebration. Located conveniently in the bustling heart of Jayanagar Housing Society Layout, Subramanyapura, our superstore was born with a simple commitment: to offer families the complete traditional and contemporary fancy experience under a single, grand roof.

From the authentic ringing chime of handcrafted Firozabad glass bangles to regal North and South Indian bridal chooda sets, every tray in our 8 expansive aisles is thoughtfully curated. Whether you bring your wedding Kanjeevaram silk saree for custom colour matching or need birthday gifts, premium cosmetics, or pooja accessories, our master staff assists you with genuine care and patience.
