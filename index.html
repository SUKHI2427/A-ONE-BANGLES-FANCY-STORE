A neighbourhood shop with a sparkle of its own

A-ONE started the way good local shops do: with a counter, a few trays of bangles, and families from the layout who kept coming back. Every festival, every wedding, every school function, someone in Subramanyapura picked up something here.

Today the shelves hold far more than bangles. Jewellery, cosmetics, gifts and daily fancy items sit side by side, so one visit covers the whole list.

We still hold bangles up to the light, still match colours to sarees, and still tell you honestly what suits you.
